  - tag: Experiment
    headline: Write an approval policy and run it in observe-only mode
    body: Before an agent approves anything, write down which changes it could approve and let it shadow human reviewers for a week. The disagreements show where the policy is wrong.
    steps:
    - 'Pick one active repository and list its common change types: docs, test-only, dependency bumps, config, schema migrations, auth code.'
    - Sort each into approve alone, approve after review agent findings are clear, or always needs a person. Keep migrations and auth on the human list.
    - Commit the policy to the repository and run it observe-only, whether in Claude Approvals, a review bot or a script that comments its verdict.
    - After a week, compare its verdicts with what human reviewers did, and promote at most one category to real approvals.
    measure: Agreement between policy verdicts and human decisions per category, reverts in categories it would have approved, and median wait for review. High agreement and no reverts in a category earns that category real approval rights.
