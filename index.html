Biography

I'm an engineering manager at AWS in Seattle, where I work on Amazon Quick. My team builds the desktop app, which brings its AI agents to your own computer. Before that we built Quick Research, the deep-research agent.

I've been at Amazon since 2016. I started on supply chain optimization, then spent seven years on Just Walk Out, the technology behind checkout-free stores. There I went from running computer vision models at scale, in the cloud and on in-store devices, to leading the teams that built its machine learning platform. Along the way I co-wrote ORL, a paper on benchmarking reinforcement learning on problems like bin packing and vehicle routing.

At home I'm teaching skills to my robotic arm, and writing about it as I go. I have a master's in computer science from NYU.
